---
title: Settings
description: Change shortcuts and label color, or pause KeyHop in an app.
---

Choose **Settings…** from the crosshair menu, or run `open -a KeyHop` while KeyHop is open.

## Shortcuts

| Action | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Click | <kbd>⌃J</kbd> |
| Select and copy | <kbd>⌃⇧J</kbd> |

Click a shortcut, then press its replacement. It must be a letter with at least one of <kbd>⌃</kbd>, <kbd>⌥</kbd>, or <kbd>⌘</kbd>, and it can't match the other action. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to keep the current shortcut.

## Label color

Click **Label color** to pick any color. KeyHop switches the label text between black and white to stay readable.

## Pause in an app

If KeyHop's shortcut clashes with an app, turn it off there:

1. Switch to the app.
2. Click the crosshair in the menu bar.
3. Choose **Pause in** followed by the app's name.

Both shortcuts stop working in that app and keep working everywhere else. Choose **Enable in** followed by the app's name to turn them back on.

## Live preview

Click inside **Live preview** to try your keys without leaving Settings. KeyHop's own shortcuts don't run on the Settings window, so use the preview to practice.
