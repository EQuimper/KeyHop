---
title: Click any point
description: Use the grid to click places that have no label.
---

Some windows have targets KeyHop can't label, such as a canvas or a game. The grid reaches any point in the window.

1. Press <kbd>⌃J</kbd>, then <kbd>Space</kbd>. The window splits into nine numbered areas.
2. Press <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>9</kbd> to zoom into an area. <kbd>1</kbd> is the top left and <kbd>9</kbd> is the bottom right.
3. Repeat until the center of the highlighted area is on your target.
4. Press <kbd>Return</kbd> to click the center, or <kbd>⇧Return</kbd> to right-click.

| 1 | 2 | 3 |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| **4** | **5** | **6** |
| **7** | **8** | **9** |

You don't need to wait for the labels. <kbd>Space</kbd> works while KeyHop is still finding controls, and when it reports that it found none.

The grid opens only when you press <kbd>Space</kbd>.
